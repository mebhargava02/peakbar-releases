What's new in PeakBar 1.0.4

🔔 You'll know the moment an update is ready

PeakBar now shows a banner notification the second a new version is found — no more clicking "Check Now" or wondering if you're behind.

Dropped from once a day to once an hour, so new features and fixes reach you the same day they ship.

Everything else stays 100% on-device.
